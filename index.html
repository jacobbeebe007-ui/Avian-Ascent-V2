⛔ Abandon Run?

This will end your current run. Any unlocks you've earned will be saved, but your progress will be lost.

🗑 Erase Saved Progress?

This permanently deletes your run save, bird unlocks, run history, highscores, and personal bests. Accessibility and music settings are kept. Cached game assets are not affected — use Clear cached data for that.

Clear Cached Data?

This removes offline cached JavaScript, CSS, and other game assets (service worker and browser cache). Your saves, unlocks, and settings are kept. The page will reload to fetch fresh files.
